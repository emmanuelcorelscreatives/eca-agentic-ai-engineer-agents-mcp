# s07-l09: reference reasoning

The explicit fallback drafts a manual-review action, attributed to the actual
application failure policy and the failed specialist/reason. It preserves the
healthy specialist result and unresolved status. It recovers no product facts and
sends nothing. This distinct recovery route satisfies the fallback objective
without silently presenting invented cached policy or a failed lookup as success.

Only TransientLookupError is retried, with an explicit maximum of three read attempts. Permanent schema errors are not retried. Timeout cancellation awaits cleanup. Missing specialist facts remain unresolved.
