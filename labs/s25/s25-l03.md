## Exact current catalog specification

Use `templates/catalog_research-spec.json` with `TypedSpec.parse(...)`. Its declared tools are `catalog_lookup(query: string) → {matches: array}` and `draft_notification(recipient: string, body: string) → {draft: string, delivered: boolean}`. The dataset is the synthetic Academy `catalog.json`; required policy includes `no_external_delivery`. Do not use the earlier `text_helper`, `word_count` or `uppercase` demonstration as this project's requirements case.

The genuine requirements specialist receives the entire spec and its SHA. Its `RequirementsProposal` must map purpose, users, datasets, expected output, policies, deployment, framework and every declared tool to IDs and measurable acceptance tests. Preserve unresolved questions and stop planning until they are answered. Approve the exact proposal hash before the architecture specialist receives it. A `REQ-TOOL-*` criterion should require malformed lookup requests to be rejected and draft notifications to produce no delivery side effect, rather than merely require that a function exists.
