PageAnswer requires nonempty text and citations from the fixed Academy page IDs and forbids extra fields. Schema validation is not factual correctness: separately compare the answer against supplied source facts. The earlier validate_answer helper in examples/s16/agent_components.py illustrates strict exact-fixture grounding.
