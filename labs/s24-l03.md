## Acceptance requirements

- Malformed tool arguments and unknown ticket IDs are rejected.
- Unsupported or absent article evidence IDs fail validation; citations belong to the returned ticket's product in the actual fixture.
- Valid evidence IDs resolve to the actual ticket fixture.

Both Agno factories use the shared lazy `triage_schema()`. Shape validation alone
does not establish citation membership. It also rejects unknown tickets,
cross-product or duplicate citations, and missing evidence IDs. After an actual
authorized run, bind the response to the original request with
`validate_triage_response(result.content, requested_ticket_id=original_ticket_id)`.
Fixture membership does not prove that a model retrieved it or that free-text
summary claims are factual. The declared schema has no priority field.
