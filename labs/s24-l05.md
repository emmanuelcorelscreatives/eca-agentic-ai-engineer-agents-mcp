## Acceptance requirements

- Final evidence references belong to actual specialist contributions.
- A missing specialist result becomes an explicit incomplete outcome.
- Team tools remain read-only and traces expose each contribution.

Inspect `specialist_completion_summary(result)` on the actual retained
`TeamRunOutput` or its JSON. Native Team `COMPLETED` means the coordinator
finished; a delegation's `tool_call_error=False` does not prove member success.
The retained member response can still have status `ERROR` and its own reported
failure content. Show those native records unchanged and mark missing,
non-completed or empty required specialist records as `incomplete`; do not force
an exception or rewrite the framework status. Even
`specialist-records-complete` establishes recorded completion only, not evidence
quality or citation approval.
