# Academy inventory and support-policy fixture continuity

These stages use one explicitly synthetic Academy shop. Stage21 stock and delivery
checks use ECA-KEY-01/P-KEY (Practice Keyboard,12in stock) and ECA-MOUSE-02/P-MOUSE
(Practice Mouse,0in stock). Stage22 support tickets and policy records carry the
same product_id and sku pairs. Policy lookup and stock availability remain
separate facts: a damaged delivered keyboard can require a review even when other
units remain in stock. No real account, customer or commercial order is represented.

Earlier planner/workbook labels such as company research, course lookup or market
server were proposed domains, not distinct PDF-mandated features. Replace their
labels in shared workbook text with inventory/stock or support-policy labels when
integrating; preserve ADK tools/sessions/MCP/A2A and Strands/Pydantic functionality.
This author owns no shared workbook and has not silently edited its source.
