## Practical exercise

Compare transient timeout recovery, exhausted fallback, invalid data and intervention outcomes.

## Acceptance and reference reasoning

Expected domain errors are captured in state and routed over native retry/finish/fallback/intervention edges. Retry is bounded. Cached synthetic fallback is labeled as such; it never becomes a successful live lookup. Intervention asks for data review rather than inventing a result. This graph does not itself implement a resumable human interrupt.
