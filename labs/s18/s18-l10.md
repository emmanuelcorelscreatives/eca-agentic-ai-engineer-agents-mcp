Resume from checkpoints rather than restarting a long-running task from the beginning.

## Practical exercise

Inject a failure after persisted progress, restart in a new process and inspect which native nodes/effects run again. Compare terminal validation and unknown-effect outcomes.

## Acceptance and reference reasoning

Recovery resumes durable graph progress instead of restarting every completed step. A local transaction/ledger can deduplicate mock effects. A terminal validation error remains terminal; an uncertain effect outcome remains unresolved and must not be automatically resent. Local mock semantics are not a claim of distributed exactly-once external delivery.
