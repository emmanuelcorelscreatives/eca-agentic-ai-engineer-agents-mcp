## Practical exercise

## Acceptance and reference reasoning

Human authority is represented by trusted application decision input, not a model approving itself. The decision binds proposal digest and reviewer fixture. Only the downstream bounded mock tool may act after accepted approval; rejection and unresolved review dispatch nothing. The fixed fixture reviewer is not a production authentication system.
