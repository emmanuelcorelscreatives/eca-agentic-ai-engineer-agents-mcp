# s20-l04: Build the Task Evaluator

Compare current progress with the objective and determine whether to continue, revise or stop.

## Coherent practical case

An Academy-owned local workshop/catalog research task, with synthetic draft-form interactions only. Keep planning, browser observations, source-attributed findings, evaluation, approvals, artifacts and user-facing progress in one persisted session.

## Source map

Project root: `projects/06-browser-sidekick`. Baseline source anchors: `advanced.py: evaluate, Evaluation, Decision; SessionController`. Read the current integrated module listed in the stage workbook as well; baseline helpers alone do not prove full application integration.

## Exercise

Compare findings against the original requested result. Produce CONTINUE, REVISE, COMPLETE and STOP outcomes for deliberately incomplete, contradicted, complete and exhausted cases.

## Acceptance and reasoning

An evaluator is useful only when its result affects the persisted execution lifecycle. Missing source evidence cannot become COMPLETE because an assistant says so. Revision preserves completed/unknown work and respects remaining replan/step budgets; unsupported or exhausted work stops honestly.

Read `projects/06-browser-sidekick/pipeline.py` → `SidekickApplication.evaluate, revise` (references prefixed advanced or ui_gradio name their neighboring module).

The integrated evaluator reads completed attributed observations and expected step predicates, then persists a lifecycle decision. revise reserves its own bounded planning attempt and preserves completed evidence. Missing evidence and unknown effects cannot be promoted by model assertions.
