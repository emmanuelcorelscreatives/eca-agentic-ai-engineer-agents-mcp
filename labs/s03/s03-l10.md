`EVALUATION_CASES` supplies grounded, unsupported, malicious-extra-permission, no-consent and matching-consent cases. `evaluate_controls` compares actual deterministic decisions with expected actions and produces per-case records; only central execution will make these observed results. It explicitly leaves subjective relevance, tone and semantic injection resistance NOT_SCORED.
